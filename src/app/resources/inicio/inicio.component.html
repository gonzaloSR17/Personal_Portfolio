About me

I consider myself patient and always face problems with a smile, looking for the best solution without losing my composure. I have worked in a computer warehouse, where I gained valuable skills in inventory management and teamwork. I always try to encourage those around me, regardless of their age. Empty criticism does not affect me, since what I truly value are the pieces of advice that help me improve and move toward a better version of myself.

We are strategists, designers and developers. Innovators and problem solvers. Small enough to be simple and quick.
